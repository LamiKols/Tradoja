About Geographical Indications

Geographical Indications (GI) are signs used on products that have a specific geographical origin and possess qualities or reputation due to that origin. Nigerian GI products represent the unique characteristics of their production regions.

Benefits of GI Certification:
  • Protected designation and branding
  • Premium market positioning
  • International recognition
  • Consumer trust and authenticity
Key Requirements:
  • Specific geographical origin
  • Traditional production methods
  • Quality linked to terroir
  • Community-based production
