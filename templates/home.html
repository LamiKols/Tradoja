About Tradoja

Tradoja is Nigeria's inclusive digital marketplace, built by Alpha Intel Advisory Ltd to connect farmers, aggregators, buyers, and traders — regardless of what phone they use.

  • Digital Inclusion: Access via Web, SMS, or USSD — works on any phone, any network.
  • Zero-Stock Trading: SabiBuy lets anyone become a trader and earn without holding inventory.
  • AI-Powered Protection: Our scam detection keeps the marketplace safe for everyone.
  • Smart Logistics: Verified transporters, cold chain tracking, and delivery coordination.
  • Multilingual Support: Trade in English, Yoruba, Hausa, Igbo, or Pidgin.

Vision: Tradoja is the foundation for AlphaLink — a future general marketplace that will expand beyond agriculture to connect buyers and sellers across all sectors of Nigeria's economy.
