About AgroLink

AgroLink is Africa's digital agriculture marketplace, connecting farmers, aggregators, buyers, and partners across cities, states, and borders. We make it easy for producers to access urban markets, streamline logistics, and unlock vital funding opportunities.

  • Empowering food producers in Nigeria and across Africa to reach more buyers and grow their businesses.
  • Reducing post-harvest losses and food waste by linking rural supply with urban demand.
  • Enabling governments, NGOs, and investors to foster sustainable food systems and resilient supply chains.

Launched in partnership with Lagos State's "Produce for Lagos" initiative, AgroLink is built to scale across Africa — one city, one farmer, one market at a time.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is AgroLink only for Lagos?

No. AgroLink launched its pilot in Lagos, but the platform is built for any state, city, or country in Africa. Any government, cooperative, or partner can use AgroLink to connect their farmers and markets.

Can farmers from outside Nigeria join AgroLink?

Yes! AgroLink is designed for all African farmers, aggregators, and buyers. We welcome participants from any country — simply register and start trading.

How do I bring AgroLink to my state or city?

Contact our partnership team at support@agrolink.com. We work with governments, NGOs, and cooperatives to launch AgroLink in new markets.

Is AgroLink linked to the ₦500bn Lagos Offtake Guarantee Fund?

Yes. AgroLink partners with Lagos State and other initiatives to make funding opportunities available on the platform. As AgroLink expands, we aim to offer funding access in every participating state and region.
