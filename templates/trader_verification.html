Why Verification?

Tradoja protects farmers from pure resellers who just mark up prices without adding value. As a trader, you must prove you provide at least one of these services:

  • Transport: You deliver produce from farms to markets
  • Aggregation: You collect from multiple small farmers
  • Processing: You clean, sort, or package produce
  • Storage: You provide warehousing or cold chain
  • Working Capital: You pay farmers upfront before resale
  • Quality Grading: You grade and sort by quality standards
