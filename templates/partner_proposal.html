Executive Summary

Tradoja (Trade + Oja, "market" in Yoruba) is a digital agricultural marketplace connecting farmers and buyers across Africa. We enable trade through multiple channels - SMS, USSD, WhatsApp, and Web - ensuring no farmer is left behind due to smartphone access.

Our platform addresses critical challenges in African agriculture: market access for smallholder farmers, price transparency, food security, and financial inclusion. We're seeking strategic partnerships with telecommunications companies to scale our impact.
